## Italy, September 11-17

No pictures posted yet. Briefly:

* Flew to Napoli, had pizza (which we'd failed to acquire 20 years ago when
we visited), visited two of the catacombs ([San
Gennaro](https://catacombedinapoli.it/en/luoghi/catacombs-of-san-gennaro-naples/)
is a community turnaround success story), tried to see birds, always
felt like I was about to get run over by a car.
* Train to Benevento via Caserta. Visited the grounds of the [Reggia de Caserta](https://reggiadicaserta.cultura.gov.it/en/),
which are actually quite large; I thought renting a bike would be overkill, and I didn't, but maybe I should have.
* Stayed at Benevento for [SCAM 2026](https://conf.researchr.org/home/scam-2026) and an extra half-day,
where I went to the local park. Better sidewalks, fewer cars.
* Then took the train to Rome and had non-Italian food (Indian). Thought about going to the climbing gym
but didn't.

## Montreal, September 18-20

## Food

Italy:
* [O Sole Mio](https://www.bestogoo.com/view/menu.php?menuid=7111) (Napoli): gotta have pizza
* [Pizzeria Alessandro Pascale](https://linktr.ee/pizzeriapascale91) (Napoli): good quattro formaggio pizza, after the catacombs
* [Pasticceria Lauri](https://www.instagram.com/pasticceria_lauri/?hl=en): eclairs (and other pastries)!
* [Gran Caffe Novara](https://gran-caff-novara.wheree.com/menu): didn't want a full meal, so the pizza magherita was appropriate
* Conference food: in Europe, actually good
* [La Pampanini](https://lapampanini.it/) (Benevento): always love croquettes; conference dinner was here also
* [B&B Domus Traiani](https://www.domustraiani.it/en/) (Benevento): really nice accommodation with proper breakfast too.
* [Le Scalette](https://www.leggimenu.it/menu/lescalette) (Benevento): it's Italy, so of course even for lunch they have a proper primi and secondi.
* Keralam (Rome): excellent fish thali plate, also a welcome change from Italian food.

Excellent food in Italy of course, though it's good to have a change too.
