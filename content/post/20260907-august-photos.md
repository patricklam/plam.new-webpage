# Catlins (August 2024)
* [Waterfalls in the Catlins (38/166), August 17](https://gallery.patricklam.ca/index.php?/category/2339)
* [Nugget Point and Catlins (32/79), August 18](https://gallery.patricklam.ca/index.php?/category/2340)

# Mount Olympus (August 2026)
* [Not Naoum Ridge (49/131), August 15](https://gallery.patricklam.ca/index.php?/category/2349)
* [Barbalas Ridge descent (53/156), August 16](https://gallery.patricklam.ca/index.php?/category/2346)

Here are two of the four sets from our time on Olympus this year. It was a shorter trip than last year. The objective was Naoum Ridge, but the visibility was not favourable (it was in a cloud). Then we descended by the Barbalas Ridge, except that we took the Olympus Ultra path instead of going through the woods on a track that was not really visible. The other two sets are Mytikas and the Xerolakki ascent. Mytikas is ready but will be in September's update. Xerolakki should be ready too.

I guess hiking landscapes and hiking companions are the fundamental type of photography that I've been trying to do since forever. Birds are more recent.

Also to come: North Macedonia and British Columbia.
