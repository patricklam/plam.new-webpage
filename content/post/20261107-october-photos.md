One overseas trip and one attempted hike in October; even if not on the hike, I didn't have my computer, so I couldn't process photos. In terms of photo processing, fewer photos processed in October than September, but more sets of photos; i.e. no monster photo sets. 16 processed, 11 added, though two of those I've already processed. The backlog extends to late November, so I can still keep it less than two years for now, though there are 12 days from December 2024 that I need to process, including a trip to French Polynesia.

In this post we'll find pictures from when I did the IJF Academy in Tokyo in September 2024, as well as the NZ Nationals in Rotorua in October 2024, plus a trip to Ontario/California/Winnipeg also in October 2024. There are some pictures around Wellington, a day from the TMB (2 more from that, though they are huge days, plus 3 from Grenoble), and a set for ATH to Petra.
