## Trip: Mitre Peak (Tararuas)

Went along with MP on a Wellington Tramping and Mountaineering Club trip just short of the Mitre Peak summit.
We left on Friday evening after work. I'd been to the bouldering gym before that, and the 4 hours of walking
over uneven terrain and a bunch of treefalls was kind of hard. It was clear but super windy on Saturday
and we stopped 20m (vert) short of the summit. Back to Wellington on Sunday with a stop for ice cream
and a second stop for a pie. Great views.

* [Hei](https://hei.co.nz/): yet another place with good (modern) bao and dumplings

Honourable mention: I'd already been to [Viet Street Food](https://www.vietstreetfood.co.nz/) but stopped by for a banh mi this month. Would go back.
