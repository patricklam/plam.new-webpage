# Health news

For a while in the 2000s it seemed like Big Pharma was spending lots of money in marketing and not really getting a lot of new drugs approved. Not every drug approved today is actually useful, but we have certainly seem some new drugs that are unquestionably effective, like GLP-1s. Here's another one for lowering cholesterol:

* [New Merck drug Lipfendra, better than statins](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/16/science/the-fda-approves-a-new-pill-to-slash-cholesterol-levels.html)

Also cancer vaccines (which reduce the rate of recurrences; it's recurrences that will get you) vs some of the worst types of canccer (melanoma and pancreatic):

* [Moderna and Merck with results from Phase 3 clinical trails on cancer vaccines](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/moderna-merck-breakthrough-could-usher-wave-cancer-vaccines-2026-08-19/), [WaPo article mirrored on MSN on cancer vaccines](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/a-melanoma-vaccine-was-hailed-as-a-breakthrough-which-cancers-are-next/ar-AA2cyifi)

And more about the COVID vaccines and myocarditis (overall lowers risk: even if the vaccine might cause myocarditis in a limited number of cases, COVID also causes myocarditis in a larger number of cases):

* [mRNA vaccine lined to lower risk of myocarditis in teens & young adults](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/covid-19/mrna-covid-vaccine-linked-lower-risk-myocarditis-teens-young-adults)

Greece:

British Columbia:
* [Cafe Crepe Express](https://cafecrepe.com/) (Vancouver): needed quick food before proceeding to Elfin Lakes, this was fine
* [Choices Market](https://choices.market/) (N Vancouver): pretty decent sandwiches-to-go
* [Alice and Brohm Ice Cream](https://www.aliceandbrohm.com/) (Squamish): Kiwi-style Real Fruit Ice Cream, but I think better than in NZ.
* [Sushi Umi](https://umi-lonsdale.ca/) (North Vancouver): as always, good Vancouver sushi; maybe less cheap than it used to be, but still reasonable
* [Rosemary Rocksalt Bagelry](https://rosemaryrocksalt.com/) (Richmond): Montreal-style bagels; reviews complain about the bagels being small, but Montreal-style bagels are actually supposed to be small; don't believe everything you read on the Internet
* [TORO Bar&Bar](https://www.torobarbar.ca/) (Victoria): excellent as expected for BC sushi; our server had studied at Queens in Kingston and had moved back to Victoria
* [Matadora Taperia](https://www.matadora.ca/) (Victoria): a bit too cool for us, but I enjoyed the food; quantities snuck up on us a bit.
* [Puerta Vallarta Amigos](https://pvamigos.com/) (Victoria): anyplace called "Fisherman's Wharf" feels like a bit of a tourist trap, but these tacos exceeded expectations
* [Murchie's Tea & Coffee](https://www.murchies.com) (Victoria): not really the serious tea place (try Heslaa), but the four-cheese sandwich and cake were good
* [Pho Breeze](https://www.facebook.com/p/Pho-Breeze-61579343940145/) (Sydney): last meal of the trip, an unremarkable banh mi
