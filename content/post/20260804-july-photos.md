This month we did go to Kaikōura for a long weekend, and I also went to Auckland for the Auckland Open (but didn't take many pictures). There was an enormous set of photos from a Kaikōura whale-watching tour (almost 900 photos) and I also processed a large number of Great Barrier Reef photos (747), in addition to another two sets of Tour du Mont Blanc photos.

The backlog is now up to mid-August 2024, which again is just under 2 years from today, but there is a Japan trip from September 2024 which may have a lot of photos too. One set at a time. Net addition from July 2026 is not too bad, with one more Kaikōura day and the Auckland trip still to be processed.

We have underwater photos from snorkelling at the Great Barrier Reef (rented a camera), birds from Birdworld Kuranda, and lots of pictures from an epic day with Whale Watch Kaikōura. Also got all of the Christchurch May 2026 pictures and one more TMB day.

# Kaikōura (July 2026)
* [Blenheim to Kaikōura (52/265), July 10](https://gallery.patricklam.ca/index.php?/category/2328)
* [Whale Watch Kaikōura (88/896) (!), July 11](https://gallery.patricklam.ca/index.php?/category/2331)

Quick trip to Kaikōura in July, as it turns out just after the [floods](https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/07/10/devastating-flood-event-latest-setback-for-kaikoura-road-networks/).
Fortunately the floods spared town this time, though the mountain biking
trails were taken out. Stopped by Wairau Lagoons just outside of Blenheim
on the way there, and Lake Elterwater on the way back (not processed this month).
<figure>
{{< gallery-slider dir="/img/20260804-july-photos/kaikoura" >}}
<figcaption style="text-align:center">
MP walking down the path; wet wetlands at Wairau Lagoons; 
Blenheim station platform (also Fidelio restaurant); flooded platform at
Lake Elterwater (we'd be back); fishing boat at
Kaikōura; golden hour outside King Tide; sunset at New World parking lot.
</figcaption>
</figure>

The real action was on the [Whale Watch
Kaikōura](https://whalewatch.co.nz/) trip. Actually a jetboat in
catarmaran shape. Got a winter discount; the boat wasn't empty but it
was far from at capacity. Saw a sperm whale (with cape petrel flying above),
a dozen humpbacks, a lot of whale tails, albatrosses and a Northern Giant
Petrel.

<figure>
{{< gallery-slider dir="/img/20260804-july-photos/kaikoura-whale-watch" >}}
